Where difficulty helps here

Drill the road out loud and time it. A road read once is a road forgotten.

Retrieval check

1. What sits on the right of a road row?

Correct: a size. A road row carries its number, its body and its size. A date belongs to the revisit schedule, and a score and a link belong to no row at all.

2. What does a figure carry below it?

Correct: a caption. The caption states what the figure shows. A quiz, a table and a step list are separate elements.
