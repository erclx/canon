Compass bearings

A bearing is the compass direction from where you stand to whatever you are aiming at, measured clockwise from north.

Hold the compass level and let the needle settle before reading anything off it. A bearing taken while walking or tilted reads confidently and wrong.

Three steps turn a sighted landmark into a number you can act on:

  1. Point the direction-of-travel arrow at the landmark.
  2. Rotate the bezel until the orienting arrow lines up with the needle.
  3. Read the bearing where the direction-of-travel arrow meets the bezel.

The same three steps run in reverse turn a bearing on a map into a direction to walk, which is the only reason to learn them in this order.
