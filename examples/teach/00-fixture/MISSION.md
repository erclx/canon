# Fixture

A placeholder workspace with no real subject, built to give the lesson-body
render layer something to render against.

## Starting point

Nothing. This workspace exists to exercise the component layer, not to teach
anyone.

## Success looks like

- Render one lesson through the components in `src/teach/components/` and
  commit its output

## Out of scope

- Every subject a workspace normally teaches. This one teaches nothing.
