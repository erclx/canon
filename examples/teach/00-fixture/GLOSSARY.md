# Glossary for Fixture

One entry per term the subject defines, sorted alphabetically.

- **Callout**: A block set apart from the prose to hold one sentence. First seen in 0001-alpha-element.html.
- **Caption**: The line below a figure saying what it shows. First seen in 0002-beta-element.html.
- **Chrome**: The header, footer navigation, stylesheet and scripts a lesson carries around its body. First seen in 0001-alpha-element.html.
- **Figure**: A drawing with a caption, wrapped so it scales. First seen in 0002-beta-element.html.
- **Lede**: The opening line under a lesson title. First seen in 0001-alpha-element.html.
- **Marker pair**: A pair of comments that bound a chrome region for the generator to rewrite. First seen in 0001-alpha-element.html.
- **Road**: A list of rows, each carrying a number, a body and a size. First seen in 0002-beta-element.html.
- **Stepper**: The rule set that shows one quiz question at a time. First seen in 0001-alpha-element.html.
