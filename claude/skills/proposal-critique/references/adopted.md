# Adopted and declined

The skill came out of a groundwork track that measured two shipped fixes against the questions a critique would have asked. This file records the options the track dropped. A later session extending the skill adds to it rather than re-arguing an item settled here.

A bullet's source is linked in its row of the toolkit's per-bullet ledger at `governance/ledger/skills/proposal-critique/`, outside the plugin, and this file links no source a row cites.

## Declined

Each item names the design that stays out, the reason, and what would bring it back.

### A default call from every planning skill

An authoring session questioning its own plan is the weak form Kahneman, Lovallo, and Sibony warn against when they ask for reviewers independent of the team that made the recommendation. The planning skills name the critique in one line for a one-way change or a fix with an unverified cause, and never call it.

Revisit when a replay finds a case where the on-demand form would not have been invoked in time.

### Folding it into `decision-escalate`

That skill batches preference questions. Most critique questions are answered from evidence, and only the remainder goes to the operator.

Revisit when critique passes show every question turning out to be preference.

### Running it as a forked subagent

A fork reads cold, which is the independence Kahneman, Lovallo, and Sibony ask for. It also loses the conversation, and a background fork loses the tools the operator questions need.

Revisit when a critique keeps agreeing with the session that wrote the proposal.

### A written critique section in the plan

A plan already carries `## Risks` and `## Questions`, and its validator owns the sections. The verdict goes to chat, and the operator decides what moves into a record.

Revisit when critiques are lost to compaction before anyone acts on them.

## Revisit the whole design when

- Replaying the chain that shipped two fixes and two or three past plans finds the on-demand form would not have been invoked in time
- One body cannot hold questions that differ so much per record, whether plan, fix, or triage
- A replayed case shows a cheap, reversible change still doing real harm
