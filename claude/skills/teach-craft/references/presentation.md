# Presentation reference

Read this before drawing a lesson or writing its page. These rules hold on a board and in standalone HTML alike, and the restraint section of `lesson-craft.md` still applies under them.

Most of this file is house judgment, picked by eye against drawn lessons, and each section says so. Where a rule rests on research, it names the source, and `adopted.md` beside this file lists the house choices with what each prevents.

## Citations

A claim the learner cannot trace is a claim they cannot check. A mark pointing at the section read lets them check it in one step.

- Mark each sourced claim with a numbered citation carrying the section read, such as `[1, §4.2]`, rather than a bare number or an inline link
- Close the lesson on a reference list in Harvard form, numbered to match the marks

Both are house judgment, picked over sources run inline at the foot of a lesson.

## Figures and density

A lesson's prose competes with its figures for the same attention.

- Cut the prose to a caption once a figure carries the idea, rather than describing the figure in a paragraph beside it
- Separate surfaces by a tint or by space, and keep a border only where it identifies a control
- Stack a wide figure's parts on a narrow screen rather than shrinking it or scrolling it sideways

Mayer 2023's coherence principle backs cutting prose a figure already carries. The tint over borders and the stacked figure are house judgment.

## The callout

The callout marks the one hard part, and a second callout makes the learner choose between them.

- Draw at most one callout per lesson, holding the hard part as the misconception and then its correction
- Keep the callout's color for the callout alone, so quiz feedback and notes take a surface of their own

Mayer 2023's signaling principle backs marking the hard part, and refutation text backs its order, per `explanation.md`. The count of one and the reserved color are house judgment.

## Type

- Step type by weight and by grey across heading, body, and secondary text, rather than by size alone or by a second color

This rule is house judgment, picked over a draft whose levels differed in size alone.

## Contrast

`ui-craft` carries the contrast floors for text and for a control's edge. A lesson breaks them in places a glance does not catch.

- Measure every color pair against its floor rather than judging it by eye, citation marks and a filled button's label included
- Measure each pair in every theme the lesson ships, since a pair passing in light can fail in dark
- Measure a control's edge against the tint it sits on, not only the page behind it

All three are house judgment, each from a failure a measured pass found in a drawn lesson.
