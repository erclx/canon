# Contrast arm

Read before writing the arms of a round declared at story, content, or composition. A round whose arms all share the skill's restraint cannot show whether restraint is what the operator wants, so one arm steps outside it.

## Drawing it

- Draw it from outside the read's restraint, bolder in density, color, or imagery than the other arms
- Label it `contrast arm` in its frame, since it breaks the one-property rule and the operator reads it as a different question
- Keep it one arm on top of the three to five, so the round still says which single property decided the rest

## Where it never goes

A round at layout or later, such as a palette round, takes no contrast arm. Those rounds hold composition fixed, and an arm that breaks it answers a question the round was not asked.

## Reporting it

Say whether the contrast arm won when reporting the pick. A win says restraint was the wrong default for this surface, and a loss says the restrained arms were what the operator wanted.
