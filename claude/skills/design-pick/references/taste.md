# Operator taste

Read before the first round at palette, imagery, or a whole visual direction. A round drafted from developer and SaaS defaults comes back generic, and the operator's own taste converges it in fewer loops.

## When to ask

Ask once per run, ahead of the first look round, for a few taste words such as calm, dense, or playful, and for any screenshots or images the operator likes. Both are optional.

- An answer of nothing proceeds on the existing source order, with no second ask
- Skip the question when the ask already gave direction
- Skip it for a logo or a single control decision, since it gates look rounds alone

## What a draft may take

Hand pasted images to `design-references`, which keeps them as its first source. From an operator image a draft takes mood, palette direction, and shape language. It never takes a product's artwork, logo, or text, since the operator pasted the image as taste and the structure-only line still guards third-party content.
