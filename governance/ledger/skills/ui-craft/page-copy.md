# Page copy reference ledger

The field is content design, with usability research beside it for how people read a page. The authorities are the GOV.UK writing guidelines, the Microsoft Writing Style Guide, and the Nielsen Norman Group's research articles. Each page was fetched and read on 2026-11-19.

A scoped search over those domains ran beside an open one, whose results were marketing guides the scoped pages outrank.

The reference came out of a measurement rather than a complaint. Over half of canon's landing sentences opened on an article or on the product name and explained the mechanism, against 22% or under on nine landing pages the operator liked, and those pages opened on a verb or on the reader.

## Declined

Candidates read and left out, so a later extension does not re-derive them.

- A rule on sentence length or on short sentences in a row. The measurement found landing pages the operator liked running both shorter and longer than canon's, so cadence did not separate them.
- A first-screen element budget, filler verbs, the staccato title, one copy register, and an outcome per section. Each already sits in `design-taste`.
- Active voice and the marketing word list, which `write-human` states for every surface.
