# Teach craft explanation ledger

The field is educational psychology, with instructional design for the goal. Every quote was copied from the source as read on 2026-11-20. Two sources were reached through copies, and each row names the copy read. Mayer was read in the University of New Hampshire's hosted PDF, and the meta-analysis in Europe PMC's full text.

The reference labels one bullet as house judgment, so its row names the failure it prevents.

## Start from the goal

| Bullet                           | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Read       |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| State the goal before content    | [Wiggins and McTighe 2012, Understanding by Design framework](https://files.ascd.org/staticfiles/ascd/pdf/siteASCD/publications/UbD_WhitePaper0312.pdf): its first stage asks "What should students know, understand, and be able to do?"                                                                                                                                  | 2026-11-20 |
| Write the check before teaching  | [Wiggins and McTighe 2012, Understanding by Design framework](https://files.ascd.org/staticfiles/ascd/pdf/siteASCD/publications/UbD_WhitePaper0312.pdf): plans "backward from long-term, desired results through a three-stage design process (Desired Results, Evidence, and Learning Plan)"                                                                              | 2026-11-20 |
| Cut what the check does not need | [Mayer 2023, Research-based principles for designing multimedia instruction](https://www.unh.edu/teaching-learning-resource-hub/sites/default/files/media/2023-06/itow-research-based-principles-for-designing-multimedia-instruction-mayer.pdf), UNH copy: "People learn more deeply from a multimedia message when extraneous material is excluded rather than included" | 2026-11-20 |

## Pair figures with words

| Bullet                       | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Read       |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| A figure paired with words   | [Pashler et al. 2007, IES practice guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/Docs/PracticeGuide/20072004.pdf), Recommendation 3: "combine graphical presentations (e.g., graphs, figures) that illustrate key processes and concepts with verbal descriptions"                                                                                                                                                       | 2026-11-20 |
| Labels on the elements       | [Pashler et al. 2007, IES practice guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/Docs/PracticeGuide/20072004.pdf), Recommendation 3: "text descriptions appear near the relevant elements in visual representations to best enhance learning"                                                                                                                                                                            | 2026-11-20 |
| The caption under the figure | [Mayer 2023, Research-based principles for designing multimedia instruction](https://www.unh.edu/teaching-learning-resource-hub/sites/default/files/media/2023-06/itow-research-based-principles-for-designing-multimedia-instruction-mayer.pdf), UNH copy: people learn more deeply "when corresponding printed words and graphics are presented near rather than far from each other on the page or screen" | 2026-11-20 |

## Refute the misconception

| Bullet                              | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Read       |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| The misconception before the idea   | [Schroeder, Kucera and Adesope 2022, Refutation text facilitates learning](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09656-z), Europe PMC full text: across "44 independent comparisons (n = 3,869)" refutation text helped | 2026-11-20 |
| Why it fails, then what replaces it | [Schroeder, Kucera and Adesope 2022, Refutation text facilitates learning](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09656-z), Europe PMC full text: refutation text has "a positive, moderate effect (g = 0.41, p < .001)" | 2026-11-20 |
| The misconception from the record   | House choice: a misconception the session imagines may be one this learner never held, so the refutation corrects nothing they believe and the real error stands                                                     | 2026-11-20 |
