# Teach craft skill ledger

The field is learning science, and the papers are the ones `adopted.md` opened through PubMed. Each row links the abstract page and quotes only what that abstract carries. The house choices stand where `adopted.md` lists a rule as house judgment, and each row names the failure the rule prevents.

The closing sections and `## What this delegates` restate these rules or route, so they take no table.

## Before teaching

| Bullet                                | Source                                                                                                                                                                  | Read       |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Ask what the learner already knows    | House choice: a lesson pitched from an assumed floor either repeats what the learner holds or skips what they lack                                                      | 2026-10-24 |
| Read the subject from sources         | House choice: a claim taught from recall is confident where it is wrong, and a learner has no way to audit it                                                           | 2026-10-24 |
| Open a returning learner on retrieval | [Roediger and Karpicke 2006, Test-enhanced learning](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16507066/): "prior testing produced substantially greater retention than studying" | 2026-10-24 |

## While teaching

| Bullet                            | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Read       |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Place each step just past unaided | House choice: a step inside what the learner can already do is review, and one far past it leaves the support doing the whole task                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | 2026-10-24 |
| Carry a quiz and a teach-back     | [Pashler et al. 2007, IES practice guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/Docs/PracticeGuide/20072004.pdf): "Use quizzing with active retrieval of information at all phases of the learning process", and [Nestojko et al. 2014, Expecting to teach](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24845756/) backs the teach-back: "Participants expecting to teach produced more complete and better organized free recall of the passage" | 2026-10-24 |
| Record the wrong answer itself    | House choice: a score tells the next session that the learner missed and not what they believe, so it places them from a count rather than the misconception                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | 2026-10-24 |
