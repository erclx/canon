# Teach craft lesson craft ledger

The reference names Butler and Roediger 2008 for feedback, Kornell, Hays and Bjork 2009 for its timing, and Nestojko et al. 2014 for the teach-back. Each row links the PubMed abstract `adopted.md` opened and quotes only that abstract.

Restraint and what makes a lesson worth returning to are editorial calls about a page, so their rows are house choices naming the failure each prevents. `## Contents` takes no table.

## Restraint

| Bullet                                   | Source                                                                                                                       | Read       |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Cut decoration carrying nothing          | House choice: an ornament competes with the material for the learner's attention and teaches nothing in return               | 2026-10-24 |
| One accent color, for the action         | House choice: a second accent splits what the eye goes to first, so the thing the learner acts on stops standing out         | 2026-10-24 |
| No animation the learner did not trigger | House choice: motion the learner did not start pulls the eye off the line they are reading                                   | 2026-10-24 |
| Whitespace over rules and boxes          | House choice: rules and boxes add marks the reader has to skip, and stacked boxes read as nesting the material does not have | 2026-10-24 |

## Quiz design

| Bullet                                 | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Read       |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Every option the same length           | House choice: a longer option reads as the considered one, so a learner picks it by length and the quiz measures test-taking                                                                                                            | 2026-10-24 |
| Rotate the correct slot by number      | House choice: an author varying the slot by judgment settles on the first one, and a learner who notices answers by position                                                                                                            | 2026-10-24 |
| Each wrong option a held misconception | House choice: an option nobody believes removes itself, so a four-option question shrinks to fewer and passes a guess                                                                                                                   | 2026-10-24 |
| One feedback block per question        | [Butler and Roediger 2008, Feedback and multiple-choice testing](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18491500/): "The selection of lures can lead students to acquire false knowledge", and feedback "reduced the proportion of intrusions" | 2026-10-24 |
| Feedback after the attempt             | [Kornell, Hays and Bjork 2009, Unsuccessful retrieval attempts enhance subsequent learning](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19586265/): "participants attempted to answer the question before being shown the answer"                   | 2026-10-24 |

## Teach back

| Bullet                                  | Source                                                                                                                                                                                          | Read       |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Explain to a named audience             | [Nestojko et al. 2014, Expecting to teach](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24845756/): "Participants expecting to teach produced more complete and better organized free recall of the passage" | 2026-10-24 |
| Ask about what the lesson was for       | House choice: a teach-back on a detail beside the point tests attention, so the learner passes it without holding the material                                                                  | 2026-10-24 |
| A hidden self-check of what to cover    | House choice: a learner alone cannot grade their own explanation, and a check shown beside the ask leaks what to say                                                                            | 2026-10-24 |
| The self-check as coverage, not a model | House choice: a model answer is read instead of produced, which turns the teach-back back into reading                                                                                          | 2026-10-24 |

## What makes a lesson worth returning to

| Bullet                            | Source                                                                                                                            | Read       |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| The worked example is complete    | House choice: a fragment forces a returning reader to rebuild the rest from memory, which is the work they came back to avoid     | 2026-10-24 |
| The lesson states what it assumes | House choice: a reader who cannot tell whether they hold the prerequisites reads on and fails at a step they were never ready for | 2026-10-24 |
| The hard part is named as hard    | House choice: material flattened to one difficulty gives a returning reader nothing to navigate by, so they reread all of it      | 2026-10-24 |
| Nothing depends on the session    | House choice: a lesson pointing at what was discussed loses its meaning once that conversation is gone, often within a week       | 2026-10-24 |
