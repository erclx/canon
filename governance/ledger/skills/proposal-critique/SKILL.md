# Proposal critique skill ledger

The field is judgment and decision-making, plus red teaming doctrine. Kahneman, Lovallo, and Sibony carry the question set and independence, Klein the premortem, Bezos the scaling, and the UK Ministry of Defence handbook the everyday red team mindset.

A row opening `House choice` states a rule no source makes. `## Output` and `## What this delegates` restate the steps or route, so they take no table.

## Guards

| Bullet                          | Source                                                                                                                                                                                     | Read       |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------- |
| Stop when no proposal is named  | House choice: a critique of a guessed proposal answers questions nobody asked                                                                                                              | 2026-10-28 |
| Critique only what is not built | [Klein, 2007](https://hbr.org/2007/09/performing-a-project-premortem): those with reservations "may speak freely at the outset, so that the project can be improved rather than autopsied" | 2026-10-28 |
| Write nothing into the record   | House choice: a plan's sections belong to its validator, and the operator decides what moves into a record                                                                                 | 2026-10-28 |

## Step 3: ask in both directions

| Bullet                          | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Read       |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| What does it solve, who hits it | [Kahneman, Lovallo, and Sibony, 2011](https://thecne.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/The-Big-Idea_-Before-You-Make-That-Big-Decision.pdf): the third group of questions is "aimed at evaluating the proposal"                                                                           | 2026-10-28 |
| Which goal, which non-goal      | House choice: a requirements record states goals and non-goals, and a proposal crossing one is decided there                                                                                                                                                                           | 2026-10-28 |
| Is the cause ours               | [UK MoD Red Teaming Handbook, 2021](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/61702155e90e07197867eb93/20210625-Red_Teaming_Handbook.pdf): the red team mindset applies "to everyday challenges and problems routinely"                                                           | 2026-10-28 |
| The strongest rival explanation | [UK MoD Red Teaming Handbook, 2021](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/61702155e90e07197867eb93/20210625-Red_Teaming_Handbook.pdf): "Team members should adopt the role of devil's advocate"                                                                               | 2026-10-28 |
| Where the numbers came from     | [Kahneman, Lovallo, and Sibony, 2011](https://thecne.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/The-Big-Idea_-Before-You-Make-That-Big-Decision.pdf): "Do you know where the numbers came from?" and "Which numbers in this plan are facts and which are estimates?"                               | 2026-10-28 |
| What doing nothing costs        | [Kahneman, Lovallo, and Sibony, 2011](https://thecne.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/The-Big-Idea_-Before-You-Make-That-Big-Decision.pdf): "Is the base case overly optimistic?"                                                                                                        | 2026-10-28 |
| Around the cause or into it     | House choice: a retry into an upstream failure repeats it, which only a question about the mechanism catches                                                                                                                                                                           | 2026-10-28 |
| Has its mechanism worked once   | [Kahneman, Lovallo, and Sibony, 2011](https://thecne.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/The-Big-Idea_-Before-You-Make-That-Big-Decision.pdf): "Were these estimates developed by adjusting from another number?"                                                                           | 2026-10-28 |

| Assume it shipped and failed    | [Klein, 2007](https://hbr.org/2007/09/performing-a-project-premortem): "the premortem operates on the assumption that the 'patient' has died, and so asks what did go wrong"                                                                                                           | 2026-10-28 |
| The cheapest reversible probe   | [Kahneman, Lovallo, and Sibony, 2011](https://thecne.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/The-Big-Idea_-Before-You-Make-That-Big-Decision.pdf): "what information would you want, and can you get more of it now?"                                                                           | 2026-10-28 |
| When to stop mitigating         | [UK MoD Red Teaming Handbook, 2021](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/61702155e90e07197867eb93/20210625-Red_Teaming_Handbook.pdf): what if analysis "imagines the different possible pathways to failure"                                                                 | 2026-10-28 |
