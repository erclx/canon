# Build in slices skill ledger

The field is software delivery practice. Google's engineering practices carry small changes, Fowler's continuous integration article carries reverting to a good build, and Addy Osmani's `incremental-implementation` skill carries the cycle and the slicing order.

The closing sections and `## What this delegates` restate these rules or route, so they take no table.

The house rows route a rule through this toolkit's own surfaces, being `git-commit`, the task's findings, and a ship chain's verify gate, where no outside source takes a side. `references/adopted.md` stays the record of what was adopted and declined at the source level.
