How a repo comes onto canon

The project starts with its stack's own tool. An agent brings it onto canon, and the same skills keep it current after every release.

canon does not make the project. bun init or npm create vite does, and canon layers on top.

Detects the stack, shows the whole chain, and writes nothing until you say yes. Then it runs canon init, checks what landed, and indexes the docs.

Reports, domain by domain, what the project holds against what canon now ships, and repairs nothing.

After a release, upgrades the CLI and plugin, previews the changelog, migrations, and drift, and on your go-ahead catches the project up.
