# Slides

The canvas every slide shares and the nine layouts a .claude/SLIDES.md deck selects through its layout field. Start with canvas.

- [Bullets layout](bullets.md): A title above one bullet list, the layout a slide with list items takes by default
- [Slide canvas](canvas.md): The margins, title band, body top, and footer every slide layout in a SLIDES.md deck draws on
- [Freeform layout](freeform.md): Author-declared text and rectangle shapes at an explicit position, size, and palette color
- [Grid layout](grid.md): A title above up to four cards in a two-by-two grid, each a term and a detail line
- [Quote layout](quote.md): A large pull quote with an attribution under it and no title band
- [Section layout](section.md): A section divider, one large heading vertically centered with nothing else on the slide
- [Stat-callout layout](stat-callout.md): A title above a row of up to four large stats, each with a caption under it
- [Title layout](title.md): The cover slide, a deck title and subtitle with no title band and no footer
- [Contents layout](toc.md): The toc slide, one linked entry per section slide in the deck
- [Two-column layout](two-column.md): A title above two labeled columns of bullets splitting the content width evenly
